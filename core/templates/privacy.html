What Information Do We Collect?

Detailed breakdown mapped directly to our operational data models.

Account Information

Username Email address Password (Hashed) Phone number WhatsApp number

We use this information to create and secure your account, communicate with you, and contact you when necessary regarding your participation in a pool. CoPay never stores or views your raw plaintext password.

Bank & Payout Information

Bank name Bank code Account number Account name

We collect bank information from users who need to receive funds or refunds from CoPay. This identifies the intended destination account and supports payout processing. Bank verification helps reduce payout errors and fraud.

Payment & Escrow Information

When you make a payment, CoPay records information necessary to identify and track the transaction, such as the transaction amount, payment reference, payment provider, escrow code, transaction status, timestamps, and dispute markers.

Important distinction: Payment providers separately process sensitive payment credentials such as card numbers, CVVs, or bank pins. CoPay does not store your card PIN or online banking password.

Payments may be processed through third-party payment providers such as Paystack and/or escrow providers such as PayScrow. Information required to complete or verify a transaction may be shared with the relevant provider.

Pool Information

We store pool details (service, host, price, user limit, duration, status, public/private visibility, and creation date) to operate shared subscription pools, calculate participant contributions, and maintain lifecycle records.

Pool Membership

Tracks who joined, their role, amount paid, payment status, access confirmation, active status, and dispute markers to determine participation and verification compliance.

⚠️ Shared Service Access Information

When a host creates a shared pool, they may provide login or access information required for contributors to access the advertised service (stored securely via structured platform fields). This information is used solely to facilitate access to the pool and should not contain unnecessary personal information.

Users must not submit information that does not belong to the shared service or that they are not authorized to share.

Dispute Records

We collect dispute information (who opened it, against whom, reasons, resolutions, resolving admin, and status) to investigate complaints, maintain an audit trail, and protect participants from fraudulent activity.

Refunds & Financial Ledger

Financial records (refund amounts, references, platform commission cuts, payout earnings, and transaction logs) are retained to track payments, resolve issues, and maintain accurate transaction histories.
