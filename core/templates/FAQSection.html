Everything you need to know about CoPay.

What is CoPay?

CoPay lets people come together to share the cost of subscriptions and other services. Instead of one person paying the entire cost alone, the cost can be split between multiple contributors.

Do I have to wait for a pool to fill before I get access?

No. You don't necessarily have to wait for every available seat to be filled. Once your payment is confirmed and the host has provided the required access, you can use the service while the pool remains open for other contributors to join. The amount associated with a pool can also vary depending on when you join.

Why can the amount or value of a pool change over time?

CoPay uses a time-based release system for subscription pools. The host's available balance is calculated based on the pool's duration and how much time has passed. The balance you see is a virtual release balance — it represents what has become available under the pool's rules.

What happens if a host doesn't complete the promised subscription period?

If a host ends the pool early or fails to provide the service for the full promised duration, contributors can raise a dispute. The host's final amount may be reduced based on time actually provided, with eligible funds handled according to resolution rules.

What is the difference between a public and private pool?

Public pools can be discovered through the CoPay Marketplace, while private pools are shared directly by their host through a private link. Both allow multiple people to contribute toward the same pool.

What happens to the money after I pay?

Your contribution is recorded against the pool and held according to the pool's payment and release rules. The host does not simply receive everyone's money immediately; CoPay tracks the balance and determines when funds become available for release.
